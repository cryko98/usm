ed. note — added to the lexicon after the timeline ran out of ways to say it.

  1. interjection

    What you say when a chart goes vertical and every other word has left the building.

    “did you see it?”  “utm.”
  2. adjective

    Describing a position that has gone up too much to be mentioned out loud.

    “not saying the number. it’s just utm.”
  3. verb · utm’d, utm’ing

    To go up too much. Mostly overnight. Mostly while you were asleep.

    “woke up and it had utm’d.”
  4. origin

    Solana timeline, 2026. A contraction of up too much, formed the same way its ancestors were: by people typing too fast to finish the sentence.

    see also FOMO, WAGMI · opposite of COPE
